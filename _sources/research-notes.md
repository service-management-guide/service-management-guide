## ITIL 5 (verified October 2026)

- <https://www.peoplecert.org/browse-certifications/it-governance-and-service-management/ITIL-1/itil-foundation-bridge-version-5-4158> (read 20 October 2026): the ITIL Foundation Bridge (Version 5) is "a fast, focused route for ITIL 4 certification holders". Topics listed: key enhancements (new and refined concepts, terminology, structure); digital product and service management (how ITIL reflects modern digital and AI-enabled environments, across the lifecycle); updated language and core concepts; an "enhanced value creation approach" linking strategy, delivery, and measurable outcomes across the stakeholder ecosystem; "managing digital and AI complexity". Exam facts on the page: 20 multiple-choice questions, 30 minutes, 65% pass mark, renewal every three years. The page gives no release date for ITIL 5.
- Secondary, **not citable** for claims (training and consultancy blogs, aggregator news): reports of a February 2026 formal release, the name "ITIL (Version 5)", a separate AI Governance publication, and shares of retained and new content. Check each on peoplecert.org before use. Wikipedia's ITIL article says PeopleCert announced ITIL 5 in January 2026 (unverified against the owner).
- `skills/itil-5/SKILL.md` (copied) summarizes the same emphasis: product orientation, AI governance, sustainability. It warns that public descriptions are mostly promotional.
