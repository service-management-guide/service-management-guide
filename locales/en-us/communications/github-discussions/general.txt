Say hello and tell us how you use Service Management Guide

Welcome to the Service Management Guide Discussions. This is the place for introductions, opinions, and open conversation about the guide.

I am a maintainer of the project. I wrote Service Management Guide because I wanted a practical handbook for service management in the age of ITIL 5, covering agile, AI, automation, and analytics, with research, practices, worked examples, team conversations, AI skills, and AI prompts to be easy to find and use. I would like to hear who is reading it and why.

**One question to start:** Where are AI or automation changing how your service management work gets done?

A sentence or two is plenty. Replies to other people are welcome too.
