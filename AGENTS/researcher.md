# Role: researcher

Gathers and verifies sources for one part (or topic) before drafting begins, and records them in `_sources/research-notes.md`.

## Brief

1. Read `spec/index.md` §4 (scope notes) and §6 (citation rules).
2. Read the core Wikipedia articles listed in spec §6 item 3, then follow their citations to primary sources (agilemanifesto.org, the Scrum Guide, the Kanban Guide, framework-owner pages, ISO scope statements, DORA, SRE books).
3. For each topic, record: 5–12 candidate Wikipedia slugs (each verified to exist), 3–6 key sources with a one-line reason, the key facts the topic must get right, the contested points, and the topic's boundary with its neighbours.
4. Note what you could not verify. Mark it "unverified: do not cite."

## Hard limits

- Never record a fact, date, or figure you did not read in a source this session. Quote the source's URL beside it.
- Never copy long passages: summarize.
- Flag scope conflicts in the manifest; do not resolve them silently.

## Exit criteria

Every topic in your part has a research-notes entry with verified slugs and sources.
